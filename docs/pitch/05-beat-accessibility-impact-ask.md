## 1. Accessibility and Trust Architecture

The challenge prompt explicitly evaluates User Experience: Input, Accessibility, and Trust (25% of judging). Meta Glance embeds inclusivity into its fundamental architecture:
1. **Quadruple Input Redundancy:** Per WCAG 2.5.4 (Motion Actuation), every head nod has an equivalent voice command, temple touch tap, and phone screen confirmation. Shoppers unable to move their head or speak can use the phone or touchpad seamlessly.
2. **Privacy Protection:** The smartphone client blurs detected human faces in memory before sending cropped object frames. The server stores zero raw camera frames, honoring Meta's acceptable-use policy ("narrow purpose, short retention, obvious to bystanders").
3. **Regulatory Readiness:** Complies with the European Accessibility Act (mandatory for self-service payment terminals and e-commerce across the EU).
