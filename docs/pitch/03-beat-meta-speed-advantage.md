---

## 1. The Strategic Pitch Thesis

The core business value for Meta is deployment velocity. Hardware adoption fails when it requires retailers to rebuild their physical stores. Meta Glance achieves 0-to-1 adoption in days because:
1. **Zero store retrofit:** No overhead ceiling cameras, no shelf weight sensors, and no bespoke scanning hardware.
2. **Standard POS compatibility:** Retailers already run self-checkout kiosks (e.g. NCR, Diebold Nixdorf, S Group). The kiosk only needs to render a lightweight web view or dynamic QR code.
3. **Consumer-owned compute:** The customer's glasses capture the image, and their phone executes the privacy filtering and network transfer.
