## Beat 1: The Everyday Friction (00:00 - 00:30 | 30s)

### Scene Direction (`scene_01_friction`)
- **Camera:** First-person point of view (POV) walking into a busy European supermarket.
- **Visual:** Hands are visibly loaded holding a shopping basket and groceries. The camera looks down as the user struggles to pull a smartphone from a pocket with one finger. A product slips. Transition to a close-up of a touchscreen self-checkout kiosk screen showing confusing touch buttons and glare.
- **Audio Ambience:** Supermarket murmur, basket handles clattering, soft ambient retail music.

### Spoken Voiceover (00:00 - 00:30 | 65 words)
> "Everyday tasks still tie down our hands. In grocery stores, shoppers juggle baskets, fumble with smartphones, and struggle with barcode scanners. For millions of blind and motor-impaired people, self-checkout kiosks and printed nutrition labels are completely unusable. Retailers tried store ceiling camera grids, but the astronomical installation costs and frequent errors forced them out. Wearables should solve this, yet today they merely mirror our phones."

## Beat 2: Hero PoC - Hands-Free Grocery Shopping (00:30 - 01:15 | 45s)

- **Visual:** The user reaches out and picks up an oat milk carton. The camera locks on the packaging. The screen flashes soft green with "Payment Approved".
- **Audio Cues:** Clear, low-latency synthetic whisper in open-ear audio: *"Oatly Oat Milk, 2.45 EUR"*. Crisp confirmation earcon on head nod. Terminal chime on kiosk payment.

### Spoken Voiceover (00:30 - 01:15 | 99 words)
> "Meet Meta Glance: our hands-free shopping protocol for Meta AI glasses. The rule is simple: glance tells, nod buys. You look at an item. The glasses immediately whisper the product name and price into your ear. A natural head nod confirms your purchase and adds it to your digital cart. Both hands stay completely free. At checkout, you walk up to a self-checkout kiosk, look at the dynamic QR code on the display, and nod once more. Payment settles instantly. No phones to unlock, no barcodes to search for, and no touchscreens to navigate. That is true hands-free utility."
