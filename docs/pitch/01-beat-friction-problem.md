# Beat 1: The Everyday Friction and Retail Problem

- Presentation Timing: 0:00 - 0:30 (30 seconds)
- Spoken Word Budget: 65 words (~135 words per minute)
- Primary Artifact: Slide 1 and Raylight Video Scene 1
- Key Theme: Everyday tasks force manual phone interaction; retail scanning and self-checkout kiosks fail shoppers and exclude people with disabilities.

---

## 1. Narrative Core and Problem Context

Everyday activities remain trapped behind manual touch interfaces. When shopping, a customer holds a grocery basket in one hand and items in the other. Adding a phone or barcode scanner causes physical friction, dropped products, and delayed lines.

For the 30 million blind and low-vision individuals in Europe, the friction is total exclusion:
- 83 percent cannot read shelf packaging or expiration dates independently.
- Touchscreen self-checkout kiosks lack tactile feedback or operable speech output, creating a barrier at the point of sale.

Prior infrastructure attempts failed:
- Ceiling camera tracking grids (e.g. Amazon Just Walk Out) proved economically unviable: costing hundreds of thousands of dollars per store with high error rates.
- Handheld scanning guns simply shift store labor onto the customer's busy hands.

## 4. Voiceover Script

> "Everyday tasks still tie down our hands. In grocery stores, shoppers juggle baskets, fumble with smartphones, and struggle with barcode scanners. For millions of blind and motor-impaired people, self-checkout kiosks and printed nutrition labels are completely unusable. Retailers tried store ceiling camera grids, but the astronomical installation costs and frequent errors forced them out. Wearables should solve this, yet today they merely mirror our phones."

## 5. Presenter Live Speaker Notes

- Hook the audience immediately with physical empathy: everyone has dropped an item while fumbling for a phone in a store.
- Emphasize that this is not an inconvenience; for blind and motor-impaired people, it is a complete barrier to independent living.
- Dismiss the ceiling camera alternative quickly: Amazon already retreated from Just Walk Out in 2024 because the economics failed.
- Establish the core question: How do smart glasses solve this without adding another screen to stare at?
