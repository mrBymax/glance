# Meta Glance: Real-Life (IRL) Shot List for Video Demo

This checklist provides the exact physical shots to film with a smartphone or camera. These external third-person shots intercut with first-person POV renders, Raylight AI sequences, and screen captures of the web kiosk and mobile app.

## Production Kit & Props Needed
- **Hardware:** Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 glasses, Meta Display with neural wristband, Android phone, laptop running `apps/website` kiosk screen.
- **Retail Props:** Grocery shopping basket, packaged items (Oat Milk carton, rye bread, apple), shopping bag.
- **Location:** Supermarket aisle or realistic hackathon table set up as a grocery shelf and checkout station.
- **Lighting:** Bright, even ambient lighting. Avoid harsh backlighting behind the laptop screen.

---

## Shot-by-Shot Production Ledger

### Beat 1: The Everyday Retail Friction (0:00 - 0:30)
*Goal: Show the physical awkwardness of phone fumbling when hands are full.*

#### Shot 1.1: The Overloaded Shopper (Wide / Medium)
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 seconds
- **Framing:** Medium shot at waist level.
- **Action:** Actor walks forward carrying a loaded shopping basket in one hand and holding two items in the other. Actor tries awkwardly to fish a smartphone out of their front pocket using only two fingers.
- **Detail to Capture:** Physical struggle, items shifting precariously, natural facial frustration.
- **Edit Cut:** Intercuts with first-person Raylight POV showing the dropped phone perspective.

#### Shot 1.2: Kiosk Hesitation (Medium)
- **Duration:** 3 to 5 seconds
- **Framing:** Side profile showing actor standing in front of an inaccessible checkout kiosk.
- **Action:** Actor balances items against their chest while tapping a glare-heavy touchscreen with a free knuckle, waiting for unresponsive touch buttons.
- **Detail to Capture:** Contrast between physical burden and clumsy digital interface.

---

### Beat 2: Hero PoC - Grocery Shopping ("Glance Tells, Nod Buys") (0:30 - 1:15)
*Goal: Show how clean, discreet, and fast the interaction looks from the outside.*

#### Shot 2.1: The Natural Inspection (Medium Profile)
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 seconds
- **Framing:** Medium close-up, eye level, 45-degree profile.
- **Action:** Actor wearing Ray-Ban Meta glasses picks up an Oat Milk carton from the shelf, holding it naturally at chest height. Actor looks directly at the carton for 1 to 2 seconds (listening to the private audio prompt in their ear).
- **Detail to Capture:** Subtle hardware capture LED visible on the glasses front frame. No hands on phones; hands stay on the product.

#### Shot 2.2: The Affirmation Nod (Tight Profile / Close-Up)
- **Duration:** 3 to 4 seconds
- **Framing:** Close-up on the actor's head and glasses.
- **Action:** While still holding the item, actor executes a single, clean downward nod of agreement. Actor immediately places the item smoothly into their basket.
- **Detail to Capture:** The nod is natural, not exaggerated. Show the open-ear speaker area on the glasses temple.

#### Shot 2.3: Approaching the Kiosk (Tracking Medium Shot)
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 seconds
- **Framing:** Tracking shot following the actor walking up to the laptop acting as the checkout kiosk.
- **Action:** Actor carries their basket directly up to the screen without reaching into pockets.

#### Shot 2.4: Glance-to-Pay Kiosk Nod (Over-the-Shoulder)
- **Duration:** 4 to 5 seconds
- **Framing:** Over-the-shoulder shot showing both the back of the actor's head wearing glasses and the laptop screen displaying the large dynamic session QR code.
- **Action:** Actor looks at the QR code, pauses for a beat, and gives a crisp nod. The screen transitions instantly to green "Payment Complete". Actor smiles and picks up their bag.
- **Detail to Capture:** Seamless handoff: zero physical touch on the computer keyboard or screen.

### Beat 3: Meta's 0-to-1 Speed Advantage (1:15 - 1:55)
*Goal: Show that no store infrastructure or ceiling modifications are needed.*

#### Shot 3.1: The Normal Store Environment (Tilt-Down Wide Shot)
- **Duration:** 4 to 5 seconds
- **Framing:** Camera points straight up at an ordinary ceiling (standard fluorescent tubes, air vents, zero surveillance cameras), then tilts down to show an ordinary retail table.
- **Action:** Visual emphasis that the ceiling is completely untouched.
- **Edit Cut:** Intercuts with 3D graphic contrasting Amazon's complex ceiling camera rigs against Meta Glance's software-only footprint.

#### Shot 3.2: The Minimal Setup (Close-Up on Hardware)
- **Duration:** 3 to 5 seconds
- **Framing:** Tabletop macro shot.
- **Action:** Camera slowly slides past the Ray-Ban Meta glasses sitting next to an ordinary smartphone and a laptop monitor.
- **Detail to Capture:** Proves the entire ecosystem consists of hardware already in consumer hands and standard store hardware.

---

### Beat 4: Future Horizon - Neural Band Handshake (1:55 - 2:35)
*Goal: Show humanity's oldest agreement gesture sealing digital peer payment.*

#### Shot 4.1: The Encounter (Wide to Medium Two-Shot)
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 seconds
- **Framing:** Two actors walking toward each other in a hall or outdoor plaza. Both are wearing Meta glasses.
- **Action:** They stop at natural conversational distance (under 1 meter). They make eye contact and smile.
- **Detail to Capture:** Proves proximity is bounded at physical arm's reach.

#### Shot 4.2: The Handshake Clasp (Hero Macro Close-Up)
- **Duration:** 5 to 7 seconds (film in slow-motion 60fps or 120fps if available)
- **Framing:** Tight close-up on the two hands meeting.
- **Action:** Sender's wrist visibly wears the Meta Display neural wristband (or controller band). The hands clasp in a firm, deliberate handshake. They give two gentle vertical pumps.
- **Detail to Capture:** Crisp focus on the wristband resting against the forearm skin as the hand grips.

#### Shot 4.3: The Mutual Confirmation (Two-Shot Medium)
- **Duration:** 3 to 4 seconds
- **Framing:** Medium shot capturing both actors' upper bodies.
- **Action:** During the handshake, both hear the confirmation chime. They exchange an appreciative nod of acknowledgment and release hands naturally.
- **Edit Cut:** Intercuts with Meta Ray-Ban Display in-lens HUD rendering "+20.00 EUR Sent".

---

### Beat 5: Team and Brand Outro (2:35 - 3:00)
*Goal: Show the team behind the prototype, credible and ready for pilot.*

#### Shot 5.1: Multi-Modal Fallback (Over-the-Shoulder / Close-Up)
- **Duration:** 3 to 4 seconds
- **Framing:** Close-up on actor using temple touchpad or phone mirror buttons to demonstrate accessibility alternatives for users who cannot move their heads.
- **Action:** Proves quadruple input redundancy (nod, voice, temple tap, phone screen).

#### Shot 5.2: The Team Hero Shot (Wide)
- **Duration:** 4 to 6 seconds
- **Framing:** Frontal wide shot of the 4 team members standing together at the hackathon table, wearing smart glasses, looking directly into camera with confident smiles.
- **Action:** The team gestures toward the working hardware on the table.
- **Detail to Capture:** Authenticity of HackJunction 2026 hackers who turned an idea into a functional prototype in 36 hours.

## Filming Recommendations for the Team

1. **Shoot in 4K at 60fps:** This allows the video editor to slow down the head nods and the handshake clasp by 50% for cinematic impact without stutter.
2. **Hold heads still for 1 second before and after nods:** Give the video editor handles on each clip so transitions cut cleanly.
3. **Film 2 to 3 takes per shot:** Vary the angle slightly (e.g. side profile vs. 45-degree angle) so the editor has coverage to match the Raylight voiceover rhythm.
